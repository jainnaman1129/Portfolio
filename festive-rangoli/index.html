Execution framework

Five workstreams, run in parallel

  • Product research

    01
    • Demand, competition and margin scoring
    • Seasonality: Diwali demand window
    • Categories: rangoli mats, diya rangoli, flower rangoli
    15 screened → 10 ASINs

  • Sourcing

    02
    • Supplier identification per ASIN
    • Client freed for offline operations
    10 / 10 ASINs sourced

  • Listing build

    03
    • Keyword-indexed titles and bullets
    • Backend search terms
    • Image stack: lifestyle, infographic, dimensions, in-box, use cases
    Peak order CVR 7.07%

  • PPC architecture

    04
    • SP Auto for search-term discovery
    • Harvested terms → SP Manual keyword
    • SP Product targeting on competitor ASINs
    • Budget scaled into the sale window
    ROAS 5.18x · ACoS 19.31%

  • Account operations

    05
    • Daily order and dispatch monitoring on Easy Ship
    • Inventory tracking through peak
    • Ads paused at stock-out to avoid wasted spend
    100% sell-through
